# Rama Nine General Hospital: Emergency department / โรงพยาบาลพระรามเก้าเจเนอรัล: ห้องฉุกเฉิน

Rama Nine General Hospital has a emergency department department registered.
Equipment on site: CT scanner, MRI scanner, cath lab.
Care this hospital can support when the department is open and staffed: stroke care, heart attack care, injury care, urgent care.
Typical emergency department wait: about 25 minutes. Location: 13.7575, 100.5672.
This document lists registered capability only. Whether a bed, device or doctor is available now comes from live status.
