# Rama Nine General Hospital: Obstetrics / โรงพยาบาลพระรามเก้าเจเนอรัล: สูติกรรม

Rama Nine General Hospital has a obstetrics department registered.
Equipment on site: CT scanner, MRI scanner, cath lab.
Care this hospital can support when the department is open and staffed: preterm birth care, pregnancy care.
Typical emergency department wait: about 25 minutes. Location: 13.7575, 100.5672.
This document lists registered capability only. Whether a bed, device or doctor is available now comes from live status.
