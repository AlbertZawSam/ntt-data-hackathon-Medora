# Phra Nakhon Women and Children's Hospital: Obstetrics / โรงพยาบาลแม่และเด็กพระนคร: สูติกรรม

Phra Nakhon Women and Children's Hospital has a obstetrics department registered.
Equipment on site: none registered.
Care this hospital can support when the department is open and staffed: preterm birth care, pregnancy care.
Typical emergency department wait: about 30 minutes. Location: 13.759, 100.499.
This document lists registered capability only. Whether a bed, device or doctor is available now comes from live status.
