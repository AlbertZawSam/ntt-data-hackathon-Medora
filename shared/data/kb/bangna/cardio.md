# Bang Na City Hospital: Cardiology / โรงพยาบาลบางนาซิตี้: อายุรกรรมหัวใจ

Department registered at Bang Na City Hospital: Cardiology.
Equipment on site: CT scanner, MRI scanner, cath lab.
Care this hospital can support when the department is open and staffed: heart attack care.
Typical emergency department wait: about 20 minutes. Location: 13.669, 100.604.
This document lists registered capability only. Whether a bed, device or doctor is available now comes from live status.
