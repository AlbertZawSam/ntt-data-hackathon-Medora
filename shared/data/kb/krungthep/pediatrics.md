# Krungthep Central Medical Centre: Pediatrics / ศูนย์การแพทย์กรุงเทพกลาง: กุมารเวชศาสตร์

Krungthep Central Medical Centre has a pediatrics department registered.
Equipment on site: CT scanner, MRI scanner, cath lab.
Care this hospital can support when the department is open and staffed: children's admission.
Typical emergency department wait: about 45 minutes. Location: 13.77, 100.542.
This document lists registered capability only. Whether a bed, device or doctor is available now comes from live status.
