# Sathorn Heart Institute: Emergency department / สถาบันหัวใจสาทร: ห้องฉุกเฉิน

Sathorn Heart Institute has a emergency department department registered.
Equipment on site: CT scanner, cath lab.
Care this hospital can support when the department is open and staffed: heart attack care, injury care, urgent care.
Typical emergency department wait: about 25 minutes. Location: 13.7235, 100.531.
This document lists registered capability only. Whether a bed, device or doctor is available now comes from live status.
