# Chao Phraya Riverside Medical Centre: Obstetrics / ศูนย์การแพทย์ริมเจ้าพระยา: สูติกรรม

Chao Phraya Riverside Medical Centre has a obstetrics department registered.
Equipment on site: CT scanner.
Care this hospital can support when the department is open and staffed: preterm birth care, pregnancy care.
Typical emergency department wait: about 30 minutes. Location: 13.7262, 100.5148.
This document lists registered capability only. Whether a bed, device or doctor is available now comes from live status.
