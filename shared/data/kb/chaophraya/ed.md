# Chao Phraya Riverside Medical Centre: Emergency department / ศูนย์การแพทย์ริมเจ้าพระยา: ห้องฉุกเฉิน

Chao Phraya Riverside Medical Centre has a emergency department department registered.
Equipment on site: CT scanner.
Care this hospital can support when the department is open and staffed: injury care, urgent care.
Typical emergency department wait: about 30 minutes. Location: 13.7262, 100.5148.
This document lists registered capability only. Whether a bed, device or doctor is available now comes from live status.
