# Chao Phraya Women's and Children's Hospital: Emergency department / โรงพยาบาลแม่และเด็กเจ้าพระยา: ห้องฉุกเฉิน

Chao Phraya Women's and Children's Hospital has a emergency department department registered.
Equipment on site: CT scanner.
Care this hospital can support when the department is open and staffed: injury care, urgent care.
Typical emergency department wait: about 25 minutes. Location: 13.7385, 100.506.
This document lists registered capability only. Whether a bed, device or doctor is available now comes from live status.
