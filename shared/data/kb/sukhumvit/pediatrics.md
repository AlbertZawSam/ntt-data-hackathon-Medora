# Sukhumvit International Hospital: Pediatrics / โรงพยาบาลนานาชาติสุขุมวิท: กุมารเวชศาสตร์

Sukhumvit International Hospital has a pediatrics department registered.
Equipment on site: CT scanner, MRI scanner.
Care this hospital can support when the department is open and staffed: children's admission.
Typical emergency department wait: about 20 minutes. Location: 13.731, 100.567.
This document lists registered capability only. Whether a bed, device or doctor is available now comes from live status.
